# *AstroLabs | hardware + software solutions

We build robust systems at the intersection of physical hardware and cloud-scale software. Inspired by the precision of a spacecraft charting a course through uncharted orbits, we engineer stable, high-performance architectures designed to bridge the physical-digital divide.

🛠️ Hardware: Embedded systems, custom firmware, and physical infrastructure.

💻 Software: Cloud-native architecture, system automation, and scalable APIs.

---

## What we build

Our first product, **Translefy**, is a live translation service — speech to translated text/audio, in real time, for anyone in the room. It's the proving ground for how we ship: people-first, ready before wide, quality over scale.

Hardware is a deliberate next chapter — once the software foundation is solid, we extend the same engineering discipline into embedded systems and physical infrastructure.

## How we operate

- **People first** — build for individuals and small groups before institutions.
- **Ready before wide** — a system earns scale by being solid first, not by shipping fast and fixing later.
- **One board, one story** — delivery work lives in GitHub Issues/Projects; strategy lives in our internal docs.
